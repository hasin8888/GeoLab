Geometric Problem Statement

Given Conditions / Setup

  • Let $ABC$ be a triangle with incircle of radius $r$.
  • Let $P$ be a point on side $BC$ such that $BP = m = 4$ and $PC = n = 3$.
  • Let $AP = u = 5$ be the cevian from $A$ to $P$.
  • The cevian $AP$ splits $\triangle ABC$ into $\triangle ABP$ and $\triangle ACP$, with inradii $r_2 = 0.9$ and $r_3 = 0.8$ respectively.

Definitions of Parameters

$r$ = inradius of the full triangle $ABC$
$r_2$ = inradius of sub-triangle $ABP$
$r_3$ = inradius of sub-triangle $ACP$
$u$ = length of cevian $AP$
$m$ = length of segment $BP$
$n$ = length of segment $PC$

Proof Goal

$$\displaystyle \frac{1}{r_2} + \frac{1}{r_3} \;=\; \frac{2}{r} + \frac{u(m+n)}{m\,n\,r}$$
