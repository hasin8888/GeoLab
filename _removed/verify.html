Statement Verifier

Give a diagram (named points, e.g. A, B, C, P) and a statement — an equation. It's checked numerically. This cannot prove a theorem, but it can disprove one instantly: if the two sides disagree for even one diagram, the statement is false as written.

Try an example

One diagram: {"A": [0,4], "B": [0,0], "C": [6,0]}
e.g. [{"A":[0,4],...}, {"A":[1,5],...}]
Named shortcuts, e.g. {"r": "inradius(A,B,C)", "m": "dist(B,P)"}. Functions: dist(P,Q), area/perimeter/inradius/circumradius(P,Q,R), angle(P,Q,R) [at Q, degrees].
Plain English can't be auto-checked here — formalize it first, or discuss it in chat.
